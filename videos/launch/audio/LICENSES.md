## Sound effects

The sound effects come from the bundled HyperFrames media-use library (`chime`, `click`,
`click-soft`, `error`, `ping`, `pop`, `whoosh-short`), sourced from Pixabay under the Pixabay
Content License (free for commercial and non-commercial use, no attribution required). Files named
`<sound>-p<n>.mp3` are the same sounds resampled by `n` semitones by `shared/authoring/sfx-cues.mjs`.
See `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`.
