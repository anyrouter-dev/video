# weekly: the look

A weekly news page: "This Week on AnyRouter" as a nameplate, the page turning section by
section. Warm newsprint where model-launch is a dark stage and free-roundup a hardware board.

**Colour.** Paper `#F3EEE2`, ink `#1B1916`, muted `#5C564C`, tiles `#E8E1D1`, one red
`#A8261B` for the dateline, the section kickers, the FREE tag and the day dots.

**Type.** Newsreader (OFL, variable) for the nameplate, headlines, the odometer numeral and
the names; Inter for datelines, labels and tags. Nothing under 32 px.

**Layout.** The nameplate stays at the top between a double and a heavy rule, with the week
number and the span of dates. Below it, one section at a time: the week as seven calendar tiles
(a red dot per model created that day, a ring per changelog entry); the number of the week, the
models in the catalog created that week, on an odometer with its split into free, BYOK and
credits; those models as rows with a FREE, BYOK or CREDITS tag (free first, as the voice names
them); the week's changelog entries, ADDED or RETIRED; then `anyrouter.dev/changelog`.

**Motion.** The rules draw, the nameplate rises. Each section turns in from the right like a
page and turns away to the left. Tiles press in one by one with their dots; the odometer rolls
up to the number; rows slide in. The camera pushes in slowly for the whole film.

**Sound.** A CC0 lo-fi beat (about 65 bpm, warm, low) that steps down 10 dB under the voice.
Page turns (two CC0 recordings, alternating) on each section, a soft click per tile rising in
pitch across the week, a pop as the odometer settles, a notification chime at the close.
Voice: Kokoro `bf_emma`, British English. -14 LUFS, true peak under -1 dBTP.
