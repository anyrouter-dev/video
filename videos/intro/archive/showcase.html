How it's made

Cut to the music

The score is synthesized in Python. Every 2-second bar is a scene, the 429s buzz, the toggle clicks, the free models ring in, and the token counter lands on the drop at 24s.

Measured match cuts

The opening lockup flies into the corner badge on exact measured coordinates. The router hub carries straight from the orbit into the routing diagram.

Dither, drawn live

An 8×8 Bayer matrix thresholds a moving field on a 480×270 canvas each frame: the background, the spinning pool sphere, the orange flood and every scene wipe.
