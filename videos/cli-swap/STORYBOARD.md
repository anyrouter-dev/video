## Video direction

- **One object, one shot.** The whole film is one Anatomy figure (`tools/figure.mjs`): a microscope on a bench console, drawn in the kit's dark ramp. The lamp in the foot is the AnyRouter key, the slide on the stage is the model, the objectives on the revolving nosepiece are the agents, the console screen is the terminal. The camera moves between shots of it; there are no cuts.
- **One accent.** `lit` (white) goes on the part to watch: the lamp while it comes on, then the objective in the light path, the slide on "model", the engraved names on "and more".
- **The swap.** The command on the screen changes one word in place, and the turret turns: a spring with a slight overshoot, the ring's ticks passing the index mark on the fixed cover, the detent ball dropping into its notch. The lamp and the slide never move.
- **Type.** Geist Mono for everything the machine shows (the screen) and for the readout; Geist for captions. Nothing under 32px while it is shown: the screen's text only appears in shots where it renders at 32px or more.
- **Chrome.** Anatomy card corners: "Fig. 1" and the title at top left, the live readout (agent and model) at top right.
- **Captions.** In the band under the picture: landscape y 880 to 1080; portrait 1240 to 1480.
- **Two renditions.** The camera has a shot list per rendition; `shared/authoring/portrait.mjs` writes the portrait project.
